Split PDF and Extract Pages

Extract any single page or section from a multi-page PDF into a separate document offline, keeping all annotations and edits intact.

How to Split PDF in Mac PDF Editor

  1. Open your multi-page PDF and locate the target page in the left thumbnail sidebar.
  2. Click the Extract Page icon on the thumbnail card.
  3. Choose a destination path to save the extracted page as a standalone PDF.
