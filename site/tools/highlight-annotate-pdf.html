Highlight and Annotate PDFs

Mark up contracts, research papers, and books with translucent highlights, rectangles, notes, and freehand drawings locally.

How to Highlight PDF in Mac PDF Editor

  1. Press H to activate the Highlight tool and pick your marker color in the Inspector.
  2. Drag across any sentence or paragraph to apply a translucent multiply highlight.
  3. Use Undo (⌘Z) anytime or save the annotated PDF with ⌘S.
