Reorder and Delete PDF Pages

Organize multi-page PDFs with live thumbnail previews: move pages up or down, duplicate templates, or delete unwanted pages with Undo.

How to Reorder PDF Pages in Mac PDF Editor

  1. Open the Pages sidebar on the left to view live Retina thumbnails of every page.
  2. Use the Move Up, Move Down, Duplicate, or Delete buttons on any page card.
  3. Press ⌘Z to undo any page change or ⌘S to write the final page order to disk.
