How to Edit a Scanned PDF on Mac (Free & Offline)

If you open a scanned PDF in Apple Preview, you cannot edit the text because the page is a flat image. Here is how to edit it in under 30 seconds with Mac PDF Editor:

Step 1: Open the Scanned PDF

Download and open Mac PDF Editor, then drag and drop your PDF file into the window (or press ⌘O).

Step 2: Run Offline macOS Vision OCR

Click the OCR button in the top toolbar. Within a second, Apple's on-device Vision framework recognizes every line of text and highlights it with a dashed blue box.

Step 3: Click, Edit & Save

Click any highlighted text block to open the inline editor. Mac PDF Editor automatically samples the surrounding background color from the scan so your edited text blends in seamlessly. Press ⌘S to save your updated PDF.
