Free Open-Source Alternative to Adobe Acrobat & PDF Expert

Most desktop PDF editors charge $80 to $240 per year just to edit a few lines of text, run OCR on a scan, or compress a document. Mac PDF Editor gives you those core workflows for free under the MIT license.

Why Switch?

  • 6 MB vs 1+ GB: Built with Tauri v2 and Rust instead of bloated Electron or multi-gigabyte creative suites.
  • Zero Cloud Uploads: Everything, including Apple Vision OCR and lopdf compression, runs 100% locally.
  • Cross-Platform: Runs natively on macOS (Apple Silicon & Intel), Windows, and Linux.
  • Developer & AI Ready: Includes a built-in HTTP API and MCP server so AI assistants can inspect, annotate, and export PDFs for you.
