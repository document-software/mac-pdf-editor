Sign PDF Documents on Mac

Add freehand vector signatures or insert transparent PNG signature images onto any PDF page without subscriptions or cloud accounts.

How to Sign PDF in Mac PDF Editor

  1. Open the PDF contract or form and navigate to the signature line.
  2. Choose Draw (D) to sketch a vector signature, or Insert Image to place a PNG signature stamp.
  3. Drag and resize your signature from the corner handle, then press ⌘S to bake it into the PDF.
