Annotate, Highlight, Whiteout & Sign PDFs

Every markup tool in Mac PDF Editor is baked directly into standard PDF geometry using pdf-lib so your signatures, highlights, and redactions display identically in Apple Preview, Chrome, and Adobe Reader.

  • Highlight (H): Translucent yellow or custom-color markers with multiply blending.
  • Rectangle & Whiteout (R): Draw crisp outlines or toggle Filled mode with white (#ffffff) to redact sensitive lines.
  • Freehand Drawing & Signatures (D): Smooth vector strokes with adjustable thickness and color.
  • PNG / JPG Stamps: Insert scanned signatures, company seals, or logos, then drag and resize them from the corner handle.
  • Export to PNG: Render any page, including all overlaid annotations, into a high-resolution PNG image.
