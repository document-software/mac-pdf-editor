Whiteout and Redact PDF Text

Cover confidential text, prices, or personal details in PDFs using filled rectangles or automatic background-matched text replacement.

How to Redact PDF in Mac PDF Editor

  1. Select the Rectangle tool (R) and enable Filled mode in the Inspector.
  2. Pick white (#ffffff) or your target cover color and drag over the sensitive region.
  3. Save the document (⌘S) to permanently render the cover rectangle into the PDF.
